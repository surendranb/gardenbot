Full Botanical Loop Schematics

gardenbot operates as an automated, closed-loop botanical diagnostic pipeline tracking your desk biome. The workflow spans from physical plant transpiration to AI-interpreted alerts and public statistics:

  • 1. Biome Sensors: High-precision BME680 environment chip (reads air temperature, humidity, pressure, and volatile gases), a capacitive soil moisture sensor, and room ambient microphone capture the desk's physical atmosphere.
  • 2. Python Collectors: collect.py fires on a launchd daemon twice every hour to read the Arduino ADC serial streams. Asynchronously, vision.py queries the USB lens and runs a Gemini Vision pass. All data points append cleanly to SQLite.
  • 3. The SQLite Core: garden.db stores all structured data, including telemetry logs, outdoor forecasts, visual metrics, and previous agent reports.
  • 4. The Claw Warden: The OpenClaw garden_warden agent triggers every 4 hours, executes garden_math.py to calculate Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPD) and soil drying slopes, evaluates foliage progress, and writes its final care interpretation back to the DB.
  • 5. Slack & Web Pipeline: High-priority care alerts deliver directly to the user's `#plantclaw` channel. Simultaneously, build_static.py compiles SQLite records into `data.json` and pushes the updated static dashboard to GitHub Pages.

About GardenBot

चेन्नई डेस्क बायोम — Chennai Desk Biome

GardenBot is an intelligent, low-intervention botanical companion designed specifically to monitor and manage a single Jade Succulent (Crassula ovata). The plant is housed in a self-watering terracotta pot placed on a physical wooden workstation desk in Chennai, India.

Target Plant Profile

Jades are highly resilient succulents native to southern Africa. They thrive in a soak-and-dry watering regime. Because their roots rot quickly in standing water, the self-watering system relies on a slow-wicking moisture sponge that dries out completely between cycles.

Stature Baseline 2 Stalks: 8.0 cm (Large) & 7.0 cm (Small)
Pot Dimensions 8.0 cm x 8.0 cm
VPD Range (Optimal) 0.8 kPa — 1.5 kPa (Succulent Transpiration)
Chennai Indoor Norms Temperature: 30°C — 36°C | Humidity: 50% — 75%

Diagnostic Rules

The system evaluates water requirements based on two signals: Soil moisture index (the physical resistance measured via the capacitive soil probe) and Turgidity (the visual plumpness/thickness of the leaves analyzed by vision). If the soil index is dry but the vision model confirms leaves are thick and turgid, the agent defaults to Hold Water to prevent root suffocation.
